Secure Information System: A Modified Algorithm for Advanced Encryption Standards

Sanjeev Patel · Pankaj Gupta · Richa Dhar · Bakul Singhal · Tajeshwar Grewal

Conference paper — 2010

Conference:
Third CSI National Conference on Education and Research (ConfER 2010)
Jaypee Institute of Engineering & Technology, Raghogarh, Guna, Madhya Pradesh, India

About the Paper

This research paper presents a modified approach to information security and the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES), with emphasis on secure information processing, authentication, access control, encryption, and enterprise-wide security.
